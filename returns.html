We want you to love what you ordered. This page explains cancellations, returns, exchanges and refunds at House & Heir.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel an order before it is packed. Write to sale@housenheir.com with your order ID, or use the chat on the site.
  • Cash on delivery orders cancelled before dispatch cost you nothing.
  • Once an order is shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but you may still return it as described below.

Returns and exchanges

  • You can ask to return or exchange an item within [[7]] days of delivery if it is unused, unwashed, and has its tags and packaging.
  • Exchanges for another size depend on stock. Every design is a limited edition and may not be available in the size you want. If it is not, we will refund you.
  • If we sent you a damaged, defective or wrong item, tell us within [[48 hours]] of delivery with photos or a short unboxing video, and we will replace it or refund you.
  • [[Add any items that cannot be returned, for example items altered at your request.]]
  • Return shipping: [[we arrange pickup free for defective or wrong items; for other returns the buyer pays / we arrange pickup]].

How refunds are paid

  • Online payments (UPI, card, netbanking, wallet) are refunded to the same method you paid with, within [[5 to 7]] working days after we receive and check the item, or after a cancellation is confirmed. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or UPI to an account you give us, within [[5 to 7]] working days.
  • Shipping and cash on delivery fees are [[refunded only if the item was defective or wrong / non-refundable]].

If a payment is taken but no order appears

Sometimes a payment succeeds but your screen closes before it confirms. The payment partner tells us within minutes and your order appears in My orders. If money was deducted and you see nothing after an hour, write to us with the payment details. Any amount that is not matched to an order is refunded automatically by the payment partner, usually within [[5 to 7]] working days.
