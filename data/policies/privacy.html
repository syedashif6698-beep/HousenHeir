[[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]] ("House & Heir", "we") runs housenheir.com. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other Indian laws that apply to us.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number. We verify the mobile number with a one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Order details: the items you order, your delivery address, payment method and order status. For online payments we receive a payment reference and the payment result. We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Things you ask us: fitting bookings, enquiries and chat messages you send us.
  • Saved preferences: designs you save to your list and the address you used last, so we can fill it in next time.
  • Technical data: a secure sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic server logs for security.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, verify you with an OTP, and keep your account secure.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders and fitting bookings, and to answer your enquiries.
  • To send order updates by email or SMS.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal and tax duties.

We do not sell your personal information. We send marketing messages only if you have agreed to receive them, and you can stop them at any time.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with service providers who help us run the shop: payment partners ([[Razorpay and Cashfree]]), an SMS provider for OTPs ([[MSG91 / Twilio]]), courier partners for delivery, our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Upstash), and an email service ([[Resend]]). They may process data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active and for [[8]] years after your last order for accounting and legal reasons. Enquiries and chat messages are kept for up to [[2]] years.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our cloud database and hosting providers, which may use servers outside India. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limit who can see your information.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and data (some order records must be kept by law). You can also withdraw consent for marketing, and nominate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf. Write to admin@housenheir.com and we will respond within [[30]] days.

Children

The shop is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian.
