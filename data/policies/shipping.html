Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses in India served by our courier partners. Enter your pincode at checkout. If we cannot deliver to it, we will tell you before you pay.

Processing and delivery time

  • Every design is a limited edition made in a small batch. Orders are packed within [[2 to 4]] working days of confirmation.
  • After dispatch, delivery usually takes [[3 to 7]] working days depending on your location.
  • Remote areas, festivals, weather and courier delays can add time. We will keep you updated.

Shipping charges

Shipping is [[free / ₹__]] on every order. Cash on delivery carries a handling fee of [[₹0 / ₹__]]. The exact amount is shown at checkout before you place the order.

Tracking your order

Sign in and open My orders to see each step: confirmed, packed, shipped and delivered. When your order ships, we add the courier name and tracking number there, and we email it to you.

Cash on delivery

You can pay the delivery partner in cash when your order arrives. Please keep the exact amount ready. If an order is refused or not accepted on repeated delivery attempts, we may ask for online payment on future orders.

Wrong or incomplete address

Please check your address and phone number. If a parcel comes back because of an incorrect address or because nobody was reachable, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery. A re-delivery charge of [[₹__ / none]] may apply.

Damaged parcels

If your parcel looks damaged on arrival, please record a short video while opening it and write to sale@housenheir.com within [[48 hours]] with your order ID.
